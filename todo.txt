in handling timing, time_step should be compared to dt instead of dt * cycles since we are measuing elapsed time so no need to multiply cycles or it will change unit (to cycles instead)

time based accumulator:

dt = frametime
accumulator += dt
while (accumulator >= time_step) <--- where time_step is the time one cycle takes to finish


cycle based accumulator:
dt = frametime
accumulator += dt * cycles_per_second <--- aka frequency
while (accumulator >= 1) <--- 1 for 1 tick/cycle
